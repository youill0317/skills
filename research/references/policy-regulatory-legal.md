# Policy, Regulatory, And Legal Landscape

Use this for policy, regulatory, or legal landscape research.

## Landscape Strategy

- Build a jurisdiction matrix before comparing rules: jurisdiction, governing
  source, source type, regulator/court/agency, status, effective date,
  compliance date, covered entities, thresholds, exemptions, penalties, and
  source URL.
- Search source families separately: statutes/codes, implementing regulations,
  official guidance, agency manuals/FAQs, official registers or gazettes,
  rulemaking dockets, enforcement databases, court orders, settlements, pending
  bills/rules, and standards incorporated by reference.
- Label status precisely: enacted, final, proposed, draft, guidance,
  nonbinding, stayed, enjoined, vacated, expired, repealed, superseded, or
  pending.
- Track authority hierarchy: constitutional/statutory text, regulation,
  guidance, enforcement action, court order, private contract, and commentary are
  not interchangeable.
- Check applicability per jurisdiction: territorial scope, covered activity,
  covered entity size/type, sector, effective date, grandfathering, exemptions,
  and preemption.
- Separate the law as written, regulator interpretation, enforcement posture,
  court treatment, and practical compliance norms.

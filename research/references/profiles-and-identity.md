# Profiles And Identity

Use this reference for profiles of people, organizations, public figures,
experts, authors, executives, vendors, nonprofits, projects, or institutions.

## Identity Anchoring

- anchor the entity with stable identifiers where available: ORCID, CIK, LEI,
  company registry IDs, tax/charity IDs, official profile URLs, known locations,
  employer or affiliation history, product/project names, publication venues,
  signature patterns, and archived official pages
- search for same-name and lookalike exclusions before merging records; keep
  explicit `not-this-person/entity` notes when records could be confused
- build a role timeline with role/title, organization, start/end or observed
  dates, source publication date, current/former/uncertain status, and whether
  the source is self-claimed, official, third-party, or archival

## Disclosure And Affiliation Checks

- for advocacy, nonprofit, funder, or expert-affiliation profiles, check primary
  disclosure sources such as tax or charity filings, annual reports, grant or
  sponsor pages, lobbying and political-donation records, public comments,
  testimony, advisory-board pages, expert bios, and publication or consulting
  disclosures
- treat social and web traces as identity evidence only when linked from
  official sources or corroborated independently; verified badges alone are not
  enough
- check handle reuse, impersonation, renamed/deleted accounts, archive
  timestamps, and whether a profile description is self-description or
  independently verified role evidence

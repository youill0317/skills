# Public Sentiment And Behavior Traces

Use this when evidence comes from reviews, forums, social media, app stores,
support threads, community posts, or other user-generated traces.

## Evidence Boundaries

- separate `sentiment`, `reported experience`, and `observed behavior`; do not
  treat stated intent or complaints as proof of actual usage, churn, purchasing,
  or population-wide demand
- use these sources as leads or qualitative examples unless the claim is
  specifically about what appears in those sources or the sample frame and
  denominator support a stronger inference
- triangulate important conclusions across independent source families before
  making broad claims about customers, users, or markets

## Sampling And Manipulation Checks

- record platform, query terms, date range, geography/language, product version,
  ranking/sorting method, visible denominator, and inclusion/exclusion choices
- check sampling bias: who posts, who is silent, moderation rules,
  self-selection, recency effects, complaint bias, fandom/anti-fandom dynamics,
  and channel mix
- check manipulation and duplication: incentives, review gating, astroturfing,
  brigading, bot-like patterns, copied text, syndicated posts, quoted reposts,
  and multiple posts from the same account, incident, company, or campaign
- check representativeness against stronger baselines when available: active
  users, downloads, transactions, support volume, surveys with disclosed methods,
  retention/churn metrics, telemetry, or official usage disclosures

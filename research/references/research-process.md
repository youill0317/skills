# Research Process

Use this when the agent needs more detail than `SKILL.md` provides. This file is
not a separate protocol. The governing behavior remains maximum-saturation
research into one Markdown record.

## Process Shape

1. State the exact research question, intended use, scope assumptions, evidence
   rules, and record path.
2. Identify every source family that could materially change the answer.
3. Build evidence lanes by source family, claim set, jurisdiction, version,
   dataset, counterevidence path, or provenance path.
4. Run scout searches to map terms, entities, aliases, source families, and
   obvious conflicts.
5. Retrieve direct target sources and inspect source bodies.
6. Snowball from useful sources: references, citations, authors, organizations,
   datasets, cases, releases, standards, issues, and archives.
7. Counter-search for disagreement, failure, criticism, retractions,
   supersession, incentives, and source manipulation.
8. Put every material lead, gap, inspected source, and claim decision into the
   single record.
9. Synthesize only after important claims are verified, downgraded, excluded, or
   marked insufficient.

## Saturation

Saturation is not a source count. A run is saturated only when further
authorized search is unlikely to change the main answer, confidence, or
decision.

Continue when:

- primary or authoritative sources are missing;
- source-family coverage is thin;
- material leads remain open;
- currentness or version status is unresolved;
- counterevidence has not been searched;
- source lineage or incentives could change confidence;
- final prose depends on unverified claims.

## Lead Handling

Treat every useful source as a source of new leads. Close a lead only when it
has been followed, proved duplicate, blocked by access, found out of scope,
judged low quality, or tied to a confidence downgrade.

## Prior Records

Use older local research records as leads and search history only. Refresh
their underlying sources, currentness, and confidence before relying on any
claim.

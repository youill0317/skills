# Current Events

Use this inside an already-triggered research task for current events,
fast-changing claims, court activity, filings, article updates, corrections,
denials, and latest-status checks. Do not use this reference to turn a simple
latest-status lookup into research by itself.

## Current Event Evidence Strategy

- Search the event in the user's language, English, and the local language of
  the event when different.
- Distinguish event date, publication date, and last-updated date.
- Build a short as-of timeline for fast-changing claims: event time, first
  report, source-of-claim, official response, court or filing update, and latest
  correction.
- Prefer primary documents, official statements, court records, filings, direct
  party statements, and local original reporting.
- For court material, distinguish complaint, indictment, motion, exhibit, docket
  entry, order, judgment, settlement, appeal, and amended filing; record case
  number, court, filing date, and whether the document alleges, rules, or
  resolves.
- Trace outlet claims back to press releases, records, statements, local
  reports, or source documents.
- Search for corrections, retractions, updates, denials, and later
  clarifications.
- Do not treat repeated coverage of the same press release, wire story, or
  unnamed source as independent evidence.
- Before finalizing, run a newest-first recency pass for corrections,
  retractions, denials, docket updates, and article update notes; state the
  as-of timestamp and timezone when the answer depends on freshness.

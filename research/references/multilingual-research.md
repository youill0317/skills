# Multilingual And Local-Language Research

Use this before broad English search when the claim, institution, event, law,
market, or media ecosystem originates in another language.

## Query Strategy

- Build parallel query sets in the user's language, English, and the origin
  jurisdiction's local language.
- Include native-script entity names, romanization/transliteration variants,
  official translations, informal translations, acronyms, agency names, docket
  terms, document-type terms, and local media terminology.
- Search local official records and local original reporting before relying on
  English-language summaries.

## Interpretation Checks

- Record which source language supports each claim and whether the wording is a
  direct quote, official translation, machine translation, paraphrase, or
  secondary summary.
- Treat translation differences, romanization differences, and local legal or
  institutional terms as possible causes of apparent source conflict.

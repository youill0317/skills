# High-Stakes Domain Protocols

Use this reference for clinical/medical, financial/investment, litigation or
legal-support, OSINT/investigative, security/technical diligence, procurement,
customer/UX, employment, or regulated-domain research.

These protocols add domain gates. They do not replace the core research process,
source verification, quality rubric, acceptance tests, or professional
deliverable standards.

## Cross-Domain Rule

For high-stakes domains, separate evidence summary, analyst assessment,
professional advice, and owner/SME decision. Unless the user and environment
explicitly support a professional work product, frame outputs as research
support and mark owner/SME review needs.

Do not write firm high-stakes synthesis while material frontier queue leads are
open. High-stakes records must show that domain-critical leads, governing
sources, blocked primary sources, counterevidence paths, currentness checks,
source-lineage doubts, and method/data gaps were followed, closed, blocked with
confidence effect, or marked unable to change the scoped conclusion.

## Clinical / Medical

- check current regulator guidance, product label, clinical guideline, trial
  registry, and full study text when method details matter
- check systematic reviews or guideline synthesis when available, but appraise
  underlying evidence quality rather than accepting conclusions by title
- record population, exposure/dose, comparator, endpoint, adverse events,
  contraindications, and applicability
- check publication status, retraction/expression-of-concern, preregistration,
  protocol deviations, funding, and COI
- require qualified clinical review before operational use

Do not present diagnosis, treatment, dosing, or safety instructions as advice.

## Financial / Investment Diligence

- check filings, audited statements, prospectuses, fee schedules, regulator
  actions, official market-data methodology, and original datasets
- record period, currency, accounting basis, restatements, related-party
  transactions, liquidity, leverage, concentration, and downside scenarios
- check source independence for analyst estimates, private-company claims, and
  market sizing
- require licensed financial, accounting, tax, or legal review where needed

Do not present investment, tax, or accounting advice as a final instruction.

## Litigation / Legal Support

- check jurisdiction, authority hierarchy, binding/nonbinding status, effective
  dates, procedural posture, docket status, and later history
- prefer primary legal materials: statutes, regulations, orders, docket entries,
  contracts, filings, agency guidance, and enforcement actions
- flag privilege, settlement, legal hold, client-sensitive, and investigative
  materials; limit quotation and require counsel/owner review
- distinguish allegations, pleadings, findings, settlements, judgments,
  sanctions, denials, and unresolved claims

## OSINT / Investigative Research

- preserve chain-of-custody metadata for URLs, archives, screenshots, media,
  account identifiers, timestamps, and retrieval method
- check identity disambiguation, impersonation/parody/account-takeover risk,
  handle reuse, platform manipulation, bot/coordinated behavior, and moderation
  context
- require geolocation/chronolocation/media-forensics handoff when location,
  timing, or authenticity is central and cannot be verified with available
  sources/tools
- apply privacy minimization and harm-risk review for personal or sensitive
  details
- do not infer identity, intent, wrongdoing, or affiliation from weak signals

## Security / Technical Diligence

- check official docs/API reference, release notes, changelog, supported
  versions, source or release tag, dependency/runtime versions, and
  reproducibility inputs
- check CVE/NVD, GHSA, OSV, vendor advisories, maintainer security pages,
  exploit status, patched versions, mitigations, and affected-version scope
- check issue/PR status, maintainer response, release cadence, security policy,
  and dependency posture
- require security owner review, threat modeling, or hands-on testing when the
  decision depends on exploitable risk

## Procurement / Vendor Risk

- record requirements, must-have criteria, evaluation weights, total cost,
  renewal/termination terms, support/SLA, warranty, implementation effort,
  lock-in, data export, and regional availability
- check security/compliance posture, certifications, data processing, privacy
  terms, subprocessors, incident history, financial viability, roadmap risk, and
  references/reviews
- check compatibility with existing systems and migration/switching cost
- trigger procurement, legal, security, finance, or data-owner review as needed

Do not recommend a vendor without decision criteria and unresolved procurement
risks.

## Customer / UX Research

- record research question, sampling, segment, recruitment channel, incentive,
  method, task/interview protocol, and analysis method
- distinguish anecdote, qualitative theme, survey estimate, behavioral data,
  support pattern, and causal claim
- check denominator, response rate, bias, duplicate/coordinated feedback,
  geography, language, product version, and timeframe
- require consent, privacy, de-identification, and quote handling

Do not generalize from isolated tickets, quotes, or reviews without sampling and
representativeness caveats.
